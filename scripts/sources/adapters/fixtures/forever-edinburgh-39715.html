This musical adaptation of the children’s book will delight audiences young and old with beautiful puppets, catchy tunes and an uplifting tale of self-acceptance for all the family.

Telling the story of Gertie, the littlest yak, with the curliest wool and grippiest of hooves for clip-clopping up cliffs, Gertie doesn’t want to be the littlest. She’s in a rush to grow up and be just like the big yaks. But what if there are some things that only a Gertie can do?

Performances take place on 17 and 18 October 2026, with a morning and matinee show on each day. For more information and tickets, see The Littlest Yak.
