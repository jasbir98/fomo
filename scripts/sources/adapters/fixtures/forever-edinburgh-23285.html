Come by yourself, or with a group of friends, and interact with strangers in a fun and unique way.

Skip the Small Talk will be offering “big talk” conversational prompts to get conversations started in a non-awkward way, allowing participants to enjoy a friendly, facilitated afternoon of meaningful conversations.

Thoughtful question prompts and a clear structure help strangers get to know one another. There will be ground rules in place to ensure that participants feel comfortable and safe sharing information, while still feeling in complete control of how much they divulge.

Running from 4pm to 6pm at Moonwake Beer Co in Leith, for more details and to book tickets visit Skip the Small Talk Night at Moonwake Beer Co.
