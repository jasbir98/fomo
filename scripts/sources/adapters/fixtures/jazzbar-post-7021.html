An Evening of Ella

20:00, £12 ADV or on the door Tickets Here!

Marianne McGregor is celebrating the timeless voice and legacy of Ella Fitzgerald. With a programme dedicated to Ella’s vast repertoire.

Marianne McGregor and her band promise an evening of tender ballads, exhilarating up-tempo swing and sensational vocal improvisation.

Like Ella, Marianne uses her voice as an instrument, reacting to the band, playing with melody and phrasing, and creating moments of spontaneity and musical dialogue. A joyful, vibrant and expressive evening of song and superb musicianship.

Vocals – Marianne McGregor
Guitar – Sam Parkinson
Piano – Tim Lallemant
Double Bass – Mario Caribe

Steele & Kershaw play Duke Ellington

20:30 – 22:30, £12 ADV or on door Tickets Here!

Trumpeter Colin Steele and saxophonist Martin Kershaw continue their long tradition of plundering the back catalogue of jazz greats! This time, they turn their attention to one of the greatest composers of the 20th century: Duke Ellington.

Alongside some of Ellington’s greatest hits, the band will celebrate the legendary musical partnership of his saxophonist Johnny Hodges and trumpeter Harry ‘Sweets’ Edison. Joining Steele and Kershaw for this all-star celebration are Paul Harrison (piano), Andrew Sharkey (bass) and Tom Gordon (drums).
