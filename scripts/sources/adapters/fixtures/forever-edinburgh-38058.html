Bringing together whisky lovers, leading distilleries, independent bottlers and some of the most exciting names in the industry, Edinburgh’s Whisky Festival offers guests the chance to explore hundreds of drams under one roof.

Whether you are a lifelong whisky enthusiast or simply curious to discover something new, the event is designed to feel welcoming, relaxed and full of atmosphere. Guests can meet the people behind the bottles, hear the stories behind the whisky and enjoy pours from across Scotland and beyond.

Alongside the whisky, visitors can expect live music, a dedicated whisky shop, expert-led experiences, an interactive whisky heritage exhibition and live coopering demonstrations from DIAGEO.

The festival is also proud to support My Name’5 Doddie Foundation, helping raise awareness and funds for research into Motor Neurone Disease. Through charity activities and fundraising initiatives, attendees can enjoy a fantastic day out while contributing to an important cause close to many hearts across Scotland.

With two sessions available and easy transport links to Murrayfield Stadium, Edinburgh’s Whisky Festival is a brilliant day out for locals, visitors to the city and whisky fans travelling from across the UK and overseas.
