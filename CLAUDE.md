# Fomo

Fomo (askfomo.com) is an Edinburgh-only app for free and cheap things to do and student or local deals. It's built with Next.js 16, Supabase and Vercel.

## Plans and to-dos
- `docs/README.md` indexes every plan and doc and holds the open to-do list. Read it when asked about plans, status, next steps or "what's left".
- When a plan is approved in plan mode, save a dated copy to `docs/plans/YYYY-MM-DD-<topic>.md` and add a row to the index.
- When the user finishes a to-do, or a new one comes up, update the "Open to-dos" list in `docs/README.md`.
